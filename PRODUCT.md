# Product: Hum & Bell

**User.** People who meditate, or want to, without an app, a subscription or the same three recordings every day.

**Painful job.** Finding a session of the right length, with the right amount of guidance, that doesn't feel stale, free and without signing up.

**Promise.** Pick a length and a practice; get a fresh session with a drone, bells and gentle guidance, made in your browser. Share or replay any session with its link.

**Wedge.** Search ("meditation timer online", "guided body scan 10 minutes", "binaural beats generator") lands on useful pages that start a session in one click; every session ends with a link to share.

**Why now.** Browsers synthesize good audio and speech locally; no audio files or servers are needed, so it can be free forever.

**Activation metric.** A completed session (`session_complete`).

**First revenue event.** Sponsorship, or a paid native app with offline packs (other hosting; `SUPPORT_URL` slot).

**v1 deliberately won't:** make health claims; use "Transcendental Meditation"/"TM"; teach any proprietary technique; record or upload anything; require an account.
