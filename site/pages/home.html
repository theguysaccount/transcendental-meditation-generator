    How it works

    1. Choose. A length, a practice, and how much guidance you want. Fine-tune the drone, background sound and bells if you like.
    2. Sit. Your browser synthesizes the sound live and speaks the guidance with its own voice. A timer runs, bells mark the start, any intervals, and the end.
    3. Keep or share it. Every session has its own link. Save favourites, or send one to a friend so you can sit with the same session.

    Nothing is downloaded or recorded: there are no audio files. The guidance comes from a library of {{line_count}} short, plain instructions, arranged differently each time. About the practices
