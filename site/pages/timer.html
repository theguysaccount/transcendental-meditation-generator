How to use a meditation timer well

  • Decide the length before you start, and let the bell end it. Not checking the time is half the point of a timer.
  • Use interval bells on longer sits, as a reminder to come back if you've drifted, or to change posture.
  • Keep the screen on. Sound keeps playing in the background in most browsers, but the page asks your device to keep the screen awake during a session, where that's supported.
  • Choose a sound you won't notice. A low drone or soft rain can cover background noise without asking for attention. Or have nothing at all.

The bells

The bells are synthesized in your browser from the same kind of uneven overtones a singing bowl makes, with a slightly detuned note for a gentle shimmer. Nothing is recorded or downloaded. The start bell sounds once; the end sounds twice; interval bells are softer.
